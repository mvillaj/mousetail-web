# Signed-in app (Dashboard + shell)

Scope: the signed-in app shell and every page it frames, led by the Dashboard. Mode: Operate.

Audience: the owner at the bench (phone, wet hands), on a propped tablet, and at a desk. Job: log a shot and act on the one next change. Constraints: dark and light both first-class; ES1 finishes are the colour system; Mousetail stays independent of Fellow (colourway names only, no Fellow marks or UI).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A warm, friendly consumer app that matches your machine: soft cards, plain language, one bright colour that means "press me", and that colour is your ES1's body. Refuses the brown-on-brown logbook and the generic habit tracker.
OWN-WORLD: Warm wood-tinted ground with clean cards (soft offset shadow in light, tonal lift in dark), large 20px radii, pill buttons. Action colour = the selected finish's body colour (default Sesame + Walnut). Fraunces headings, Figtree UI, Red Hat Mono readings (Spline Sans Mono was tried first and read as typewriter at small sizes). Mono for figures only, units and words in Figtree. Drawn Lucide icons.
STORY: Open the app, see the last shot as a glass and one big barista instruction in your machine's colour, pull again, log it with one thumb.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: light sidebar with icon nav and active pill; the coach card is the one filled machine-colour surface: last shot's glass, "Grind 2 steps finer", the reason, Log next shot. Beside it the dial-in strip: this coffee's shots as glasses on one fixed scale with time and signed deltas. Below: a quiet summary line, recent shots with mini glasses. Phone: bottom tab bar with a centre Log shot button.
FORM: Warm Consumer App fused with espresso, seed key 4455cff9 (challenger digital-design-canon-warm-consumer-app-surface). Signature: every shot drawn as a demitasse; liquid height = yield on one fixed scale, crema colour = taste (pale sour to dark bitter, hazel balanced). Tamp-feel press on buttons.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; ends with the finish review, the verdict and DESIGN.md.
